MANOBODHA — Website files

Files included:
- index.html: the website homepage.
- assets/manobodha-logo.png: extracted MANOBODHA logo from your business card.
- assets/manobodha-logo.jpg: logo image asset.
- assets/manobodha-business-card.jpeg: business card image asset.

To preview:
1. Keep index.html and the assets folder together.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

To publish on Netlify:
1. Sign in to Netlify and open your MANOBODHA site.
2. Open the Deploys page.
3. Drag and drop the contents of this folder (index.html and the assets folder) into the manual deploy area.

Do not upload only the assets folder. Keep the folder structure intact.
